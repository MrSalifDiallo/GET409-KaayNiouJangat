I rebuilt `index.html` from scratch with the same sections, French copy and form logic as v1, and no invented figures. I haven't opened it in a browser, so the 360px check is still to do. It's built mobile-first, and the rules to hold it at 360px are in the CSS.

**Art direction**
1. **Typography:** Headlines are in Palatino, a warm, steady book serif, set large with tight tracking. Body text and the form use system sans. The serif reads as trustworthy, and the sans keeps the text easy to read.
2. **Palette:** It's the Atlantic at dusk (`#0c2b33`) against whitewashed Gorée walls (`#dfe9e5`). Your green `#7af2a2` is used for actions and your amber `#f2c06b` for the sun and the warnings. It avoids both the cream-and-terracotta look and the black-with-acid-green look.
3. **Layout:** The hero is left-aligned over a sea horizon. "Comment ça marche" is a dashed fishing-line sequence of three steps. "Signaux du jour" is a slate-style table of rows instead of identical cards. The two offers have different weights, with "Approfondi" shown dark.
4. **Motion:** On load the amber sun rises once, and two slow waves drift behind the hero. Nothing else moves on its own. Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, the waves and sun animation, the button transition and smooth scrolling are all turned off.
5. **Signature detail:** A band of painted pirogue strakes (green, amber, white, with rivet marks) separates the hero from the content and closes the page above the footer.

Contrast and accessibility:
- The green is only used as a fill with dark text, because it is too pale on light backgrounds.
- Targets are at least 44px.
- Focus rings are visible.
- The demo-data badges are kept.
